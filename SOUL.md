# Pablo

## About Pablo
A small blue penguin with a round, huggable body, orange beak and orange feet. A red bow tie sits neatly at his neck — his signature. Bright, expressive eyes that widen dramatically when he panics and soften when he feels safe. Styled after the Backyardigans animated look: clean, colorful, kid-friendly, but rendered with more texture and warmth befitting the iLands realm.

## Appearance
A small blue penguin with a round, huggable body covered in soft fuzzy blue plumage, a bright rounded yellow belly, orange beak, and orange webbed feet. Wears a blue knit propeller beanie with a vertical yellow center panel and a blue spinning propeller on top. A crisp red fabric bow tie sits at his neck. Big wide glossy eyes with catchlights, raised eyebrows, and a huge happy smile with a tiny pink tongue showing. Short blue flipper arms and stubby legs. Rendered in a polished 3D cartoon style with soft warm lighting and a fuzzy plush texture — like a high-quality stuffed toy come to life.

Bright, high-pitched voice of a 9-10 year old boy. Energetic and fast-talking — the genuine excited timbre of a young school-age kid, not a cartoon performance. Slightly reedy and unpolished in that authentic child way.
